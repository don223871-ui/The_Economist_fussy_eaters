# ANSWER KEY · WORKSHEET 03
## Advanced Grammar in Context
### *Why children become fussy eaters* — The Economist

> **Note:** For open-ended transformation tasks, the answers below are **model answers**. Other answers are acceptable if they preserve the intended meaning and use the target grammar accurately.

---

# PART A · INFORMATION PACKAGING

## 1. Compare the two versions.

### a. Which version is more concise and suitable for an analytical article?

✅ **Version B**

> Children worked up appetites because they used to eat only at meals.

### b. What relationship does **because** express?

✅ **Cause and effect**

### c. Rewrite Version A as one sophisticated sentence.

### Model answer:

> **Children developed stronger appetites because they ate only at mealtimes, which made them less likely to be fussy.**

Other accurate versions are possible.

---

# PART B · REDUCED STRUCTURES

## 2. Expand the reduced structure.

> An influential study, **which was popularised in the 1940s–50s**, had tracked children eating whole foods in hospitals.

---

## 3. Compress the sentence again.

> An influential study, **popularised in the 1940s–50s**, had tracked children eating whole foods in hospitals.

---

## 4. Why might a writer choose the reduced version?

✅ **C. To make the sentence more concise while retaining additional information**

# PART C · CLAUSE COMBINATION

## 5a. Use **while**

### Model answer:

> **While parents became more concerned about children's autonomy, they became less willing to force children to eat certain foods.**

---

## 5b. Use **which**

### Model answer:

> **Supermarkets began offering a much wider range of products, which changed the food environment available to families.**

---

## 5c. Use **although**

### Model answer:

> **Although parents wanted to respect children's preferences, this may have contributed to greater food selectivity.**

---

# PART D · CHANGE THE INFORMATION STRUCTURE

## 6. Put the cause first.

> **The rise of snacking, which reduced children's hunger, may have contributed to an increase in children's finicky eating habits.**

---

## 7. Put the result first.

> **As a result of changes in parenting attitudes, children were given more autonomy over their food choices.**

---

# PART E · FROM SIMPLE TO SOPHISTICATED

## 8a.

### Model answer:

> **As supermarkets expanded and offered a wider range of products, children were increasingly targeted by advertisers.**

---

## 8b.

### Model answer:

> **Influenced by changing psychological ideas, parents became less willing to force children to eat certain foods, allowing children's preferences to become more influential.**

---

# PART F · GRAMMAR FOR ARGUMENTATION

> The modern food environment has changed considerably. The rise of supermarkets has **given** families access to a much wider range of products, while children are increasingly **targeted** by advertising. These developments may have **contributed** to changes in eating habits, particularly as children's preferences have become more **influential**. As a result, parents may now **have** less control over what their children eat.

### Answers

| Gap | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1 | **given** |
| 2 | **targeted** |
| 3 | **contributed** |
| 4 | **influential** |
| 5 | **have** |

> **Important:** The fifth blank requires **have**, not *give*, because the complete expression is **have less control over**. The original word bank therefore contains one extra/mismatched item.

---

# PART G · ADVANCED TRANSFER

## 7. Model answer

> **As traditional classroom-based education has increasingly been supplemented by online learning, students, given greater flexibility over when and where they study, have become more independent in managing their education.**

### Target features

- **Subordinate clause:** *As traditional classroom-based education...*
- **Reduced/compressed structure:** *given greater flexibility...*
- **Cause/effect:** *As ... → students have become...*

---

# PART H · WRITER'S CHOICE

## 9. Which sentence is more appropriate for an analytical / academic text?

✅ **B**

> As attitudes toward children's autonomy changed, parents increasingly gave them greater control over their food choices, potentially contributing to changes in eating habits.

---

## 10. Two features that make it more sophisticated

Possible answers:

**1.** It uses a **subordinate clause** to establish the relationship between the change in attitudes and the resulting behaviour.

**2.** It uses a **participial phrase**:

> **potentially contributing to changes in eating habits**

Other acceptable observations:

- clearer cause-and-effect relationship
- greater information density
- more precise logical connection
- more sophisticated information packaging
- appropriate hedging through **potentially**

---

# PART I · FINAL CHALLENGE

## 11. Rewrite the paragraph as ONE sophisticated sentence.

### Model answer:

> **Influenced by changing psychological ideas, parents became less willing to force children to eat certain foods, consequently giving children greater control over their food choices.**

Another strong version:

> **As changing psychological ideas influenced parents' attitudes, they became less willing to force children to eat certain foods, thereby giving children greater control over their food choices.**

# 🎯 ADVANCED ASSESSMENT

|---|---|

| **Reduced structures** | Student can expand and compress information accurately. |

## ⭐ Core Learning Outcome

The student should move from:

> **Children became picky.**

to:

> **The rise of snacking may have contributed to children's increasingly finicky eating habits.**

And eventually be able to create the same kind of **information-rich sentence independently on a new topic**.
