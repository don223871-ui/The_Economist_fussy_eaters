# 🟢 LEVEL A2
## Basic Grammar Patterns

### 1.

> Children **eat** different foods, but some children are very fussy.

✅ **Answer: eat**

---

### 2.

> Some children may be hungry less often **because** they eat snacks between meals.

✅ **Answer: because**

---

# 🟡 LEVEL B1
## Intermediate Grammar Patterns

### 3.

> Children **have gained** more control over their food choices in recent decades.

✅ **Answer: have gained**

**Pattern:**

> **have/has + past participle**

---

### 4.

> Children may eat less at mealtimes if they **have** too many snacks.

✅ **Answer: have**

**Pattern:**

> **if + present simple → may + verb**

---

# 🟠 LEVEL B2
## Upper-Intermediate Grammar Patterns

### 5.

> Children are increasingly **targeted** by food advertisers.

✅ **Answer: targeted**

**Pattern:**

> **be + past participle**

---

### 6.

> The rise of snacking may have **contributed** to changes in children's eating habits.

✅ **Answer: contributed**

**Pattern:**

> **may have + past participle**

---

# 🔵 LEVEL C1
## Advanced Grammar Patterns

### 7.

> An influential study, **popularised** in the 1940s and 1950s, influenced later parenting advice.

✅ **Answer: popularised**

**Pattern:**

> **past participle phrase**

Full form:

> An influential study, **which was popularised** in the 1940s and 1950s, ...

---

### 8.

> **Although** parents wanted to respect children's preferences, their increased freedom may have contributed to more selective eating.

✅ **Answer: Although**

**Pattern:**

> **Although + clause, main clause**

---

# 🟣 LEVEL C2
## Proficiency-Level Grammar Patterns

### 9.

> **As** supermarkets expanded rapidly, families gained access to a much wider range of products.

✅ **Answer: As**

Here **as** introduces a relationship between the development in supermarkets and the resulting change in access.

---

### 10.

> The rise of snacking may well have **reduced** children's appetite at mealtimes.

✅ **Answer: reduced**

**Pattern:**

> **may well have + past participle**

This expresses a relatively confident but still qualified possibility.

---

# 🔴 LEVEL C3
## Extended Advanced Grammar & Information Packaging

### 11.

> **Much as** parents may have intended to give children greater autonomy, this freedom may also have contributed to greater food selectivity.

✅ **Answer: Much as**

**Pattern:**

> **Much as + subject + verb, ...**

Meaning:

> **Although / even though**

The structure introduces a strong concessive relationship.

---

### 12.

> **Having been** exposed to a wider range of processed foods and advertising, children may have become more selective about what they eat.

✅ **Answer: Having been**

**Pattern:**

> **Having been + past participle**

This is a **perfect passive participle clause**.

It presents the exposure as occurring before or leading into the main-clause situation.
