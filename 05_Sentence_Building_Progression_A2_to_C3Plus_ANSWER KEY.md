# 🟢 LEVEL A2
## BUILDING A COMPLETE SENTENCE

### 1 · DAILY ROUTINE

> **I usually study English in the evening.**

**Pattern:**  
Present Simple + frequency adverb

---

### 2 · FOOD

> **I usually drink coffee in the morning.**

**Pattern:**  
Present Simple

---

### 3 · STUDY / WORK

> **I check my emails every morning.**

**Pattern:**  
Present Simple + time expression

---

### 4 · LAST WEEK

> **Last week, I visited a friend.**

**Pattern:**  
Past Simple

---

### 5 · ABILITY

> **I can speak English quite well.**

**Pattern:**  
can + base verb

---

### 6 · PREFERENCE

> **I really like watching films at home.**

**Pattern:**  
like + -ing

---

### 7 · FAMILY / FRIENDS

> **My best friend lives in another city.**

**Pattern:**  
Subject + verb + complement

---

### 8 · HABIT

> **I sometimes go for a walk after work.**

**Pattern:**  
frequency adverb + Present Simple

---

### 9 · PLACE

> **I often go to a café near my home.**

**Pattern:**  
Present Simple + place

---

### 10 · YOUR LIFE

> **Learning English is an important part of my daily life.**

**Pattern:**  
Gerund as subject + be

---

# 🟡 LEVEL B1
## CONNECTING IDEAS

### 11 · BECAUSE

> **I study English every day because I want to communicate more confidently.**

**Target relationship:**  
Cause → reason

---

### 12 · SO

> **I was very tired, so I went to bed early.**

**Target relationship:**  
Cause → result

---

### 13 · ALTHOUGH

> **Although I was tired, I continued working.**

**Target relationship:**  
Contrast / concession

---

### 14 · WHEN

> **When I finish work, I usually go for a walk.**

**Target relationship:**  
Time

---

### 15 · IF

> **If I have enough time, I will study English tonight.**

**Pattern:**

> if + Present Simple → will + verb

---

### 16 · USED TO

> **I used to study only at weekends, but now I study every day.**

**Target:**  
Past habit → present contrast

---

### 17 · COMPARISON

> **My life is more organised than it was a few years ago.**

**Pattern:**  
comparative + than

---

### 18 · RELATIVE CLAUSE

> **I have a friend who always encourages me to keep learning.**

**Pattern:**  
noun + who + clause

---

### 19 · TWO IDEAS

Original:

> I was tired.  
> I continued working.

### Model answer:

> **Although I was tired, I continued working.**

Another acceptable answer:

> **I was tired, but I continued working.**

---

### 20 · PERSONAL CAUSE & EFFECT

> **Because I started planning my day more carefully, I became more productive.**

**Structure:**

> Because + cause → result

---

# 🟠 LEVEL B2
## CONTROLLING COMPLEX GRAMMAR

### 21 · PASSIVE

> **My emails are usually answered within a few hours.**

**Pattern:**

> be + past participle

---

### 22 · PASSIVE + TIME

> **My daily routine has recently been changed by my new work schedule.**

**Pattern:**

> Present Perfect Passive

> has/have + been + past participle

---

### 23 · MODAL PERFECT

> **I may have forgotten to reply to an important message.**

**Pattern:**

> may have + past participle

---

### 24 · REGRET / ALTERNATIVE

> **I should have started learning English more seriously earlier.**

**Pattern:**

> should have + past participle

---

### 25 · GERUND

> **I am interested in learning new ways of improving my English.**

**Pattern:**

> interested in + -ing

---

### 26 · INFINITIVE

> **I study English regularly in order to communicate more effectively.**

**Pattern:**

> in order to + base verb

---

### 27 · PARTICIPLE CLAUSE

Original:

> I was tired.  
> I went home early.

### Model answer:

> **Feeling tired, I went home early.**

**Pattern:**

> -ing participle clause + main clause

---

### 28 · CAUSE → RESULT

> **Working from home has contributed to a more flexible daily routine.**

Alternative:

> **Regular exercise has led to significant improvements in my energy levels.**

---

### 29 · COMPLEX NOUN PHRASE

> **The increase in my daily workload has changed the way I organise my time.**

**Target structure:**

> the increase in + noun

---

### 30 · B2 PERSONAL SENTENCE

> **Because I started working longer hours, I have had to reorganise my daily routine.**

**Contains:**

- dependent clause
- main clause
- cause/effect
- Present Perfect

---

# 🔵 LEVEL C1
## INFORMATION PACKAGING

### 31 · REDUCED RELATIVE CLAUSE

Original:

> I recently read an article which was published online.

### Answer:

> **I recently read an article published online.**

Full relative clause:

> which was published online

Reduced:

> published online

---

### 32 · PERSONAL APPLICATION

> **I recently watched a documentary produced by an independent filmmaker.**

Full version:

> a documentary which was produced by an independent filmmaker

Reduced version:

> a documentary produced by an independent filmmaker

---

### 33 · NON-DEFINING RELATIVE CLAUSE

> **My English teacher, who has encouraged me for several years, has had a major influence on my learning.**

**Pattern:**

> noun, who + extra information, ...

---

### 34 · CONCESSION

> **Although I was extremely busy, I made time to study English.**

---

### 35 · ADVANCED CONCESSION

> **Despite being extremely busy, I made time to study English.**

Alternative:

> **In spite of being extremely busy, I made time to study English.**

---

### 36 · NOMINALISATION

Basic idea:

> People are using social media more.

### Answer:

> **The increasing use of social media has changed the way people communicate.**

**Nominalised idea:**

> people are using social media more

↓

> the increasing use of social media

---

### 37 · INFORMATION COMPRESSION

Original:

> I started working from home.  
> My daily routine changed.  
> I became more flexible.

### Model answer:

> **Starting to work from home changed my daily routine, making my schedule considerably more flexible.**

More advanced alternative:

> **The transition to working from home resulted in a more flexible daily routine.**

---

### 38 · PARTICIPLE CLAUSE

Original:

> I had finished my work.  
> I went out for a walk.

### Answer:

> **Having finished my work, I went out for a walk.**

**Pattern:**

> Having + past participle

---

### 39 · RESULTING IN

> **I started exercising regularly, resulting in higher energy levels throughout the day.**

**Pattern:**

> main clause + , resulting in + noun phrase

---

### 40 · C1 MASTER SENTENCE

### Model answer:

> **Having changed my daily routine, I have become more productive, which has allowed me to devote more time to learning English.**

**Contains:**

- perfect participle clause
- main clause
- relative clause
- cause/result relationship

---

# 🟣 LEVEL C2
## ADVANCED SYNTACTIC CONTROL

### 41 · INVERSION

Basic ideas:

> Learning English has improved my communication skills.  
> It has also changed the way I think.

### Answer:

> **Not only has learning English improved my communication skills, but it has also changed the way I think.**

**Pattern:**

> Not only + auxiliary + subject + verb...

---

### 42 · NEGATIVE INVERSION

> **Rarely do I have enough uninterrupted time to study for several hours.**

**Pattern:**

> Rarely + auxiliary + subject + verb

---

### 43 · CLEFT SENTENCE

Basic:

> Regular practice improved my English.

### Answer:

> **What improved my English was regular practice.**

**Pattern:**

> What + clause + be + focus

---

### 44 · IT-CLEFT

Basic:

> I became more confident after I started speaking English every day.

### Answer:

> **It was when I started speaking English every day that I became more confident.**

**Pattern:**

> It was + focus + that + clause

---

### 45 · PERFECT PARTICIPLE

> **Having completed my work, I went for a walk to clear my mind.**

**Pattern:**

> Having + past participle

---

### 46 · PERFECT PASSIVE PARTICIPLE

> **Having been given more responsibility at work, I had to become better at managing my time.**

**Pattern:**

> Having been + past participle

---

### 47 · HEDGING

Basic:

> Social media has changed how people communicate.

### Model answer:

> **Social media may have significantly changed the way people communicate.**

More cautious:

> **Social media may, to some extent, have changed the way people communicate.**

---

### 48 · ADVANCED CAUSE & EFFECT

> **I started exercising regularly, thereby improving both my energy levels and my concentration.**

**Pattern:**

> thereby + -ing

---

### 49 · MULTIPLE-LAYER SENTENCE

### Model answer:

> **Having changed my daily routine, I may have become more productive, thereby allowing me to devote greater attention to my studies.**

### Components:

| Requirement | Example |
|---|---|
| Subordinate/compressed structure | **Having changed...** |
| Participle clause | **Having changed...** |
| Uncertainty | **may have become** |
| Cause/result | **thereby allowing...** |

---

### 50 · C2 MASTER SENTENCE

### Model answer:

> **It was only after I had changed my daily routine that I began to realise how significantly it could affect my productivity.**

This contains:

- ☑ cleft structure
- ☑ complex clause
- ☑ emphasis
- ☑ embedded clause
- ☑ information packaging

Another stronger version:

> **Not until I had reorganised my daily routine did I realise that the changes I had initially considered insignificant could, in fact, have a substantial impact on my productivity.**

This version demonstrates:

- ☑ inversion
- ☑ subordinate clause
- ☑ embedded relative structure
- ☑ hedging / qualification
- ☑ information density

# 🔴 LEVEL C3+
## EXPERT-LEVEL SENTENCE DESIGN

> **C3 is used here as an extended expert category, not as an official CEFR level.**

---

### 51 · FRONTED CONCESSION

> **Much as I wanted to maintain my old routine, I eventually realised that it was preventing me from making meaningful progress.**

**Pattern:**

> Much as + subject + verb, ...

Meaning:

> Although / even though

---

### 52 · FRONTED CONDITION

> **Were I to change my current routine, I would probably become considerably more productive.**

Equivalent:

> If I were to change my current routine, ...

---

### 53 · INVERTED CONDITIONAL

> **Had I started planning my time earlier, I might have avoided many of the problems I later encountered.**

Equivalent:

> If I had started planning my time earlier, ...

---

### 54 · ADVANCED PARTICIPLE CLAUSE

> **Having been given greater responsibility at work, I had to reorganise my daily routine, which ultimately made me more efficient.**

**Structure:**

> Having been given...

↓

prior experience

↓

> I had to reorganise...

↓

consequence

---

### 55 · MULTIPLE INFORMATION PACKAGING

Original:

> I changed a habit.  
> It was difficult at first.  
> It produced a positive result.

### Model answer:

> **Although changing the habit proved difficult at first, I eventually adapted to it, resulting in a more productive daily routine.**

---

### 56 · CLEFT + CAUSE

> **What ultimately changed my daily routine was the increasing pressure of my workload.**

Alternative:

> **It was the increasing pressure of my workload that ultimately forced me to change my daily routine.**

---

### 57 · INVERSION + EMPHASIS

> **Not until I had experienced the consequences of poor time management did I realise how important planning was.**

Normal version:

> I did not realise how important planning was until I had experienced the consequences of poor time management.

---

### 58 · HEDGED MULTI-CLAUSE ARGUMENT

### Model answer:

> **Although social media may, to some extent, have made communication more immediate, its constant use could arguably contribute to a decline in the quality of face-to-face interaction.**

### Required elements:

- **cautious claim:** may / could
- **cause:** social media use
- **consequence:** decline in interaction quality
- **qualification:** to some extent / arguably
- **concession:** although

---

### 59 · EXPERT INFORMATION PACKAGING

Original ideas:

> I started exercising regularly.  
> It was difficult at first.  
> My energy levels improved.  
> I now have a healthier routine.

### Model answer:

> **Although establishing a regular exercise routine proved difficult initially, I have since found that it has significantly improved my energy levels, thereby helping me maintain a healthier lifestyle.**

### Structure:

> Although + difficulty

↓

> main clause

↓

> that-clause

↓

> thereby + result

---

### 60 · C3+ MASTER SENTENCE

### Model answer:

> **Not until I had experienced the limitations imposed by my old routine did I realise that it was the lack of consistency, rather than a lack of motivation, that had been preventing me from making meaningful progress, despite having repeatedly convinced myself that I was simply too busy.**

### Structures:

☑ inversion

☑ cleft structure

☑ nominalisation

☑ embedded relative clause

☑ perfect structure

☑ concession

☑ multiple information layers

# ⭐ FINAL TASK
## ONE IDEA · SIX LEVELS

برای این بخش، **یک جواب واحد وجود ندارد**؛ چون دانشجو باید یک اتفاق واقعی از زندگی خودش انتخاب کند.

اما این یک **Model Progression** است:

### Core idea:

> I started exercising.

---

### A2

> **I started exercising last year.**

Simple sentence.

---

### B1

> **I started exercising last year because I wanted to become healthier.**

Cause.

---

### B2

> **Because I wanted to become healthier, I started exercising regularly, which gradually improved my energy levels.**

Complex clause + relative clause.

---

### C1

> **Having decided to improve my health, I started exercising regularly, which gradually resulted in higher energy levels.**

Perfect participle + relative clause + result.

---

### C2

> **It was my desire to improve my health that ultimately motivated me to establish a regular exercise routine, thereby improving my energy levels.**

Cleft + nominalisation + thereby.

---

### C3+

> **Not until I had experienced how negatively my sedentary routine was affecting my energy levels did I realise that establishing a regular exercise habit, difficult as it initially proved, could fundamentally alter both my physical well-being and my approach to daily life.**

Inversion + embedded clause + concessive structure + nominalisation + complex information packaging.

# 📊 TEACHER'S ANSWER-KEY MAP

| Level | What the student should demonstrate |
|---|---|
| **A2** | A complete, grammatically accurate basic sentence |
| **B1** | Two ideas connected logically |
| **B2** | Control of complex grammatical relationships |
| **C1** | Information compression and clause reduction |
| **C2** | Deliberate emphasis and syntactic manipulation |
| **C3+** | Multi-layered information packaging and stylistic control |

---

# 🎯 THE REAL LEARNING OUTCOME

The purpose is **not**:

> “Can the student use inversion?”

The deeper question is:

> **Can the student choose an appropriate structure to express a particular meaning?**

Therefore the progression is:

**A2**

> Make a sentence.

↓

**B1**

> Connect ideas.

↓

**B2**

> Express relationships.

↓

**C1**

> Compress information.

↓

**C2**

> Control emphasis.

↓

**C3+**

> Design the sentence around the intended meaning.

### Final principle

> **Grammar → Meaning → Choice → Sentence Design → Communication**

The ultimate goal is for the student to stop thinking:

> “Which grammar rule should I use?”

and start thinking:

> **“What exactly do I want to say, and which structure expresses that meaning most effectively?”**
