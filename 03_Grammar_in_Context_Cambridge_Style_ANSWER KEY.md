# SECTION 2 · B2+ → C1
## Open Cloze

### 7–12

> The rise of snacking, for example, may have contributed **7. to** a decline in children's appetite at mealtimes. At the same time, children were increasingly given more freedom to decide what they wanted to eat, partly because parents became concerned **8. about** the possible psychological effects of forcing them to eat.
>
> As supermarkets expanded, families gained access **9. to** a much wider range of products. Advertising also became more influential, with children increasingly being targeted **10. by** food companies.
>
> These developments may have contributed **11. to** a food environment in which children's preferences became more influential **12. than** they had been in the past.

# SECTION 6 · C2 PROFICIENCY
## High-Level Key Word Transformation

### 27

**DISCRETION**

> Parents increasingly **left children to their own discretion over** what and how much they ate.

### ⚠️ Better natural formulation

The original task is slightly problematic because **discretion** does not naturally fit the intended structure with *what and how much they ate*.

A more natural C2-style transformation would be:

> Parents increasingly **left it to children's discretion** what and how much they ate.

However, this is a good example of something to revise before using the item in a real Cambridge-style worksheet.

# SECTION 7 · C2 PROFICIENCY
## Advanced Sentence Construction

### 33

> **As parenting attitudes changed, parents became less willing to force children to eat, consequently giving them greater control over their food choices.**

Another strong version:

> **Influenced by changing attitudes towards parenting, parents became less willing to force children to eat, thereby giving them greater control over their food choices.**

---

### 34

> **As supermarkets expanded and advertising increasingly targeted children, children's preferences became more influential, potentially contributing to changes in their eating habits.**

This combines:

- **Cause:** supermarkets expanded / advertising targeted children
- **Qualification:** potentially
- **Result:** contributing to changes in eating habits

---

### 35 · C2 Challenge

### Model answer:

> **As children's exposure to processed foods and targeted advertising increased, their eating habits may have become increasingly selective, influenced by the greater range of choices available to them.**

### Features:

| Requirement | Example |
|---|---|
| Historical change | **increased** |
| Cause | **As children's exposure... increased** |
| Consequence | **their eating habits may have become...** |
| Uncertainty | **may have** |
| Compressed structure | **influenced by...** |

- basic collocations

# ⚠️ TEACHER'S QUALITY CHECK

Before using this worksheet as a **Cambridge-style assessment**, I would revise a few items rather than blindly treating every question as an authentic Cambridge-format question.

Most importantly:

### Question 27

The combination of:

> **DISCRETION + what and how much they ate**

is not sufficiently natural for a high-quality transformation item.

### Question 25

The answer:

> **played a role in increasing...**

is natural, but the original prompt needs careful control of the expected word count if this is intended to function as a strict Cambridge Part 4 item.

### Therefore:

This worksheet is excellent as a **Cambridge-inspired progression practice**, but if we want a truly rigorous **FCE → CAE → CPE examination-style worksheet**, the next refinement should be:

> **Every item → correct level → authentic task format → exact word limit → single defensible answer → Cambridge-style distractor logic**

That would make the worksheet substantially stronger as an assessment tool.
