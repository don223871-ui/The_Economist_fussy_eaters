# HOW TO USE THIS WORKSHEET

This is **not a traditional grammar exercise**.

You are going to build sentences about **your own real life**.

At each level:

- the grammatical pattern becomes more complex;
- the amount of information increases;
- the relationship between ideas becomes more sophisticated;
- you move from **making a sentence** to **controlling information inside a sentence**.

### Core progression

> **A2 → Make a sentence**  
> **B1 → Connect ideas**  
> **B2 → Control complex relationships**  
> **C1 → Compress information**  
> **C2 → Manipulate emphasis and information structure**  
> **C3+ → Produce sophisticated, multi-layered sentences**

# 📈 FINAL PROGRESSION MAP

| Level | Sentence-building ability | Main structures |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **A2** | Make a complete sentence | Basic clauses, tense, can, frequency |
| 🟡 **B1** | Connect two ideas | because, although, if, when, relative clauses |
| 🟠 **B2** | Control complex grammar | passive, modals, perfect forms, participles |
| 🔵 **C1** | Compress information | reduced clauses, nominalisation, complex relatives |
| 🟣 **C2** | Control emphasis | inversion, clefts, hedging, advanced participles |
| 🔴 **C3+** | Design information | layered structures, fronting, inversion, qualification |

# 🎯 THE CORE PRINCIPLE

The student should experience this progression:

### A2

> **I study English every day.**

↓

### B1

> **I study English every day because I want to communicate more confidently.**

↓

### B2

> **Having realised how important English is for my work, I have started studying more consistently.**

↓

### C1

> **Having realised the importance of English for my work, I have developed a more consistent study routine, which has gradually improved my confidence.**

↓

### C2

> **It was the realisation that English would significantly affect my professional opportunities that motivated me to develop a more consistent study routine.**

↓

### C3+

> **Not until I had experienced the professional limitations imposed by my lack of confidence in English did I realise that developing a consistent study routine, difficult as it initially proved, could fundamentally alter both my communicative ability and my professional prospects.**

## THE ULTIMATE GOAL

> **Don't learn a difficult structure just because it is difficult.**

The goal is:

**IDEA → CHOOSE THE STRUCTURE → BUILD THE SENTENCE → CONTROL THE INFORMATION → EXPRESS YOUR MEANING**

That is the point where grammar stops being a list of rules and becomes **a tool for thinking and communicating.**
