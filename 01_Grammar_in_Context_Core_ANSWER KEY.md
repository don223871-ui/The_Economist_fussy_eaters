## Grammar in Context
### *Why children become fussy eaters* — The Economist

> **Note:** Questions 8–10 and Part E are open-ended. The answers below are **model answers**; students may produce different answers if the grammar and meaning are accurate.

# PART A · NOTICE THE STRUCTURE

## 1–2. Identify the grammatical function

| Sentence | Answer |
|---|---|
| **A.** Children **made nutritious choices when left to their own devices**. | ✅ **Reduced structure / participial phrase** |
| **B.** The rise of snacking **resulting in a lack of hunger**... | ✅ **Cause/effect relationship** |
| **C.** This research **was popularised in parenting books**. | ✅ **Passive voice** |

### Key observation

**A**

> when left to their own devices

is a shortened form of:

> when **they were left** to their own devices.

**B**

> resulting in a lack of hunger

shows a **result/consequence**.

**C**

> was popularised

follows:

> **be + past participle**

and is therefore passive.

# PART B · GRAMMAR IN USE

## 3. Passive Voice

### a.

> The research **was popularised** in parenting books.

### b.

> Children **were targeted** directly by advertisers.

### c.

> More autonomy **was given** to children in some families.

---

## 4. Cause & Effect

### a.

> The rise of snacking **resulted in** a lack of hunger.

### b.

> Changes in parenting attitudes **contributed to** children having more control over food choices.

### c.

> The growth of processed food **led to** changes in children's eating habits.

> ⚠️ **Teacher note:** These answers are acceptable as language practice, but not all three cause-effect relationships are stated as direct claims in the article. The article specifically links the rise of snacking with lack of hunger and finicky eating; other relationships here are transfer exercises based on ideas discussed in the article. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0} :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

# PART C · TRANSFORM THE SENTENCE

## 5.

**Original:**

> Advertising companies began targeting children directly.

**Passive:**

> **Children began to be targeted directly by advertising companies.**

---

## 6.

**Original:**

> Snacking reduced children's hunger.

**RESULTED**

> **The rise of snacking resulted in a lack of hunger.**

This closely reflects the relationship presented in the article. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

---

## 7.

**Original:**

> Parents gave children more control over their food choices.

**AUTONOMY**

> **Parents gave children more autonomy over their food choices.**

The article specifically discusses parents entrusting children with more autonomy in deciding what and how much to eat. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

# PART E · MINI TRANSFER TASK

### Model answer:

> **Online learning has contributed to major changes in education. More courses are now offered online, and students are often given greater autonomy over when and how they study.**

| Structure | Example |
|---|---|

# 🎯 TEACHER'S QUICK ASSESSMENT

| Skill | What to look for |
|---|---|
| **Passive** | Correct use of **be + past participle** |
| **Cause & Effect** | Correct relationship between cause and result |
| **Autonomy** | Correct meaning + natural use of the collocation |
| **Transfer** | Student can move beyond the article |
| **Accuracy** | Grammar remains correct in a new context |
