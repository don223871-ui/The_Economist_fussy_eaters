### How to use this worksheet

The grammar patterns below are selected from the **language and ideas of the article**, but arranged as a **level progression**.

For each level:

- **5 target grammar patterns**
- **2 sentence-completion questions**
- increasing grammatical complexity from **A2 → C3**

# 🎯 WHAT THIS WORKSHEET MEASURES

The important point is that the student is **not simply doing twelve unrelated grammar questions**.

The same article topic is being used to test a progression:

> **A2**
> → Can I form a correct basic sentence?

> **B1**
> → Can I connect ideas?

> **B2**
> → Can I control more complex grammatical relationships?

> **C1**
> → Can I compress and organise information?

> **C2**
> → Can I express complex relationships precisely?

> **C3**
> → Can I package several layers of meaning into sophisticated English?

### Final principle

**ONE ARTICLE → SIX LEVELS → 30 GRAMMAR PATTERNS → 12 PRODUCTION ITEMS**

The article therefore becomes a **grammar progression framework**, rather than simply a source of isolated grammar exercises.
