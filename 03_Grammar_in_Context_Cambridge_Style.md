# SECTION 1 · B2 FIRST
## Use of English — Part 4 Style
### Key Word Transformations

**Level:** B2  
**Main focus:** Grammar + Vocabulary  
**Task type:** Key Word Transformation

در B2 First، Part 4 از دانشجو می‌خواهد جمله دوم را با استفاده از یک **key word** طوری کامل کند که معنی جمله اول حفظ شود؛ تمرکز اصلی Grammar و Vocabulary است. 

### 1

Children had more control over what they ate because parents gave them greater autonomy.

**AUTONOMY**

Parents gave children greater __________________________ what they ate.

---

### 2

The rise of snacking contributed to children's lack of hunger.

**RESULTED**

The rise of snacking __________________________ children's lack of hunger.

---

### 3

Advertisers increasingly targeted children directly.

**TARGETED**

Children __________________________ advertisers.

---

### 4

Parents were less willing to force children to eat certain foods.

**RELUCTANT**

Parents __________________________ children to eat certain foods.

---

### 5

Supermarkets offered families a much wider range of products.

**ACCESS**

Supermarkets gave families __________________________ a much wider range of products.

---

### 6

Some parents believed that forcing children to eat could cause psychological harm.

**CONCERNED**

Some parents were __________________________ forcing children to eat.

---

### B2 CHECK

Before moving on, check:

- Can you preserve the original meaning?
- Can you recognise the required grammatical pattern?
- Can you use the key word naturally?
- Can you control the structure without copying the original sentence?

# SECTION 2 · B2+ → C1 ADVANCED
## Use of English — Part 2 Style
### Open Cloze

**Level:** Upper B2 → C1  
**Main focus:** Grammar in context  
**Task type:** Open Cloze

Cambridge C1 Part 2 uses a text with gaps where the candidate supplies the missing word; the task tests grammatical awareness and vocabulary. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

### Complete the text with ONE word in each gap.

Changes in children's eating habits cannot be explained by parenting attitudes alone. The rise of snacking, for example, may have contributed **(7)** ________ a decline in children's appetite at mealtimes. At the same time, children were increasingly given more freedom to decide what they wanted to eat, partly because parents became concerned **(8)** ________ the possible psychological effects of forcing them to eat.

As supermarkets expanded, families gained access **(9)** ________ a much wider range of products. Advertising also became more influential, with children increasingly being targeted **(10)** ________ food companies.

These developments may have contributed **(11)** ________ a food environment in which children's preferences became more influential **(12)** ________ they had been in the past.

چرا این ترتیب؟
-
چون دانشجو از تغییر کنترل‌شده‌ی یک جمله شروع می‌کند و مرحله‌به‌مرحله به تولید مستقل و پیچیده می‌رسد:
```
B2: Transform
↓
B2+/C1: Complete
↓
C1: Reformulate
↓
C1+: Compress / Expand
↓
C2: Manipulate meaning and structure
↓
C2: Produce independently
```

این دقیقاً با progression موردنیاز Use of English هماهنگ است؛ به‌ویژه Part 4 که در B2، C1 و C2 بر transformation و کنترل Grammar/Vocabulary تمرکز دارد، با افزایش سطح در پیچیدگی و دقت lexical/structural.
  
نکته 
-
این Worksheet را عمداً به شکل B2 → C1 → C2 ladder چیده‌ام؛ بنابراین لازم نیست همه دانشجوها همه بخش‌ها را انجام دهند.

برای یک کلاس mixed-level می‌توانیم مثلاً:
```
B2 students: Sections 1–2
B2+/C1 students: Sections 2–4
C1 students: Sections 3–5
C1+/C2 students: Sections 5–7
```

را انجام دهند.

این‌طوری همه روی همان Grammar in Context کار می‌کنند، اما عمق و پیچیدگی task بر اساس سطح Cambridge تغییر می‌کند.
